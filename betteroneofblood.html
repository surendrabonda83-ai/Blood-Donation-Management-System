Health Tips for Donors

Before Donation

  • Get a good night's sleep
  • Eat a healthy meal 2-3 hours before
  • Drink plenty of water
  • Wear comfortable clothing with sleeves that can be raised

After Donation

  • Rest for 10-15 minutes
  • Drink fluids for the next 24 hours
  • Avoid strenuous activities for 24 hours
  • Eat iron-rich foods to replenish your supply

Eligibility

  • Age 18-65 years
  • Weight at least 45 kg
  • Hemoglobin level above 12.5 g/dL
  • Wait at least 56 days between donations

Benefits

  • Free health check-up
  • Reduced risk of heart disease
  • Balanced iron levels
  • The satisfaction of saving lives!
